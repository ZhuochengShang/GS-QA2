Which is at a higher elevation, [3] or [4]?
Of [3] and [4], which sits at a greater elevation?
Between [3] and [4], which one is more elevated?
Which has a higher elevation, [3] or [4]?
Of the two, [3] and [4], which lies at a greater elevation?
Which of [3] and [4] stands at the higher elevation?

-- SQL_TEMPLATE_START
-- Answer type: name
-- Local raster operation: two ST_Value lookups, one per POI coordinate anchor.
-- Returns the higher POI's display name and both elevations for evaluation.
WITH p1 AS (
  SELECT ST_Value(d.rast, 1, ST_GeomFromText('[3_wkt]',4326)) AS elev
  FROM public.dem_us d
  WHERE ST_Intersects(d.rast, ST_GeomFromText('[3_wkt]',4326))
  LIMIT 1
),
p2 AS (
  SELECT ST_Value(d.rast, 1, ST_GeomFromText('[4_wkt]',4326)) AS elev
  FROM public.dem_us d
  WHERE ST_Intersects(d.rast, ST_GeomFromText('[4_wkt]',4326))
  LIMIT 1
)
SELECT
  CASE
    WHEN p1.elev > p2.elev THEN '[3]'
    WHEN p2.elev > p1.elev THEN '[4]'
    ELSE 'equal'
  END AS higher_poi,
  p1.elev AS poi1_elevation_m,
  p2.elev AS poi2_elevation_m
FROM p1, p2;
-- SQL_TEMPLATE_END
