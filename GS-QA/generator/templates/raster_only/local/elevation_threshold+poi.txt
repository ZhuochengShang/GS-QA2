Is [3] at an elevation above [2] meters?
Does [3] sit higher than [2] meters above sea level?
Is [3] more than [2] meters in elevation?
Is the elevation at [3] greater than [2] meters?
Does [3] exceed [2] meters in elevation?
Is [3] above the [2]-meter elevation mark?

-- SQL_TEMPLATE_START
-- Answer type: yes-no
-- Local raster operation: single ST_Value lookup at the POI coordinate anchor.
SELECT
  CASE
    WHEN ST_Value(d.rast, 1, ST_GeomFromText('[3_wkt]',4326)) > [2]
    THEN 'yes' ELSE 'no'
  END AS answer,
  ST_Value(d.rast, 1, ST_GeomFromText('[3_wkt]',4326)) AS elevation_m
FROM public.dem_us d
WHERE ST_Intersects(d.rast, ST_GeomFromText('[3_wkt]',4326))
LIMIT 1;
